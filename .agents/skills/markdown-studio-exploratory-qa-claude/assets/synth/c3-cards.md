# Card titles

A numbered grid-card title keeps its number literal; the rest of the title is inline markup.

::::{grid} 2

:::{grid-item-card} 1. First *card*
Body of the numbered card.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} Second card
Body of the second card.
:::

::::

:::{card} Standalone **bold** title
Body of the standalone card.
:::
