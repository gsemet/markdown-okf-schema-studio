## Figure width and alignment show in the preview

### Normative behavior

When a writer sets a figure's width or alignment, the Studio SHALL show the figure at that width and alignment.

### Preserved stakeholder intent

As a writer, I want figure sizing reflected immediately so that I can lay out the page.

### Rationale and constraints

- Sizes and alignment follow what the source says.
