## Grid cards adapt to the pane width

### Normative behavior

When a writer lays out content with grid cards, the Studio SHALL show the cards in a layout that adapts to the pane width.

### Preserved stakeholder intent

As a writer, I want card layouts that stay usable in narrow panes so that I can design pages in VS Code.

### Rationale and constraints

- Cards reflow rather than overflow sideways.
