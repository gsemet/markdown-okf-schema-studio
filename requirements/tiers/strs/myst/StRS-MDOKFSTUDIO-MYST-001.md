## Admonitions render as coloured callouts

### Normative behavior

When a writer uses a MyST admonition of any kind, with a custom title, as a dropdown or nested, the Studio SHALL show it as a coloured callout with its title and body.

### Preserved stakeholder intent

As a writer, I want every admonition kind, custom title, dropdown and nesting to look like a distinct callout so that I can judge my document as readers will.

### Rationale and constraints

- Applies to all ten named admonition kinds, custom titles, dropdowns and nesting; the same look holds in dark and light themes.
