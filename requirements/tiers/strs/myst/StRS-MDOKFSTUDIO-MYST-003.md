## Definition lists and inline text roles are typeset

### Normative behavior

When a writer uses definition lists, subscript, superscript, abbreviations or keys, the Studio SHALL show them typeset.

### Preserved stakeholder intent

As a writer, I want these MyST text constructs typeset in the preview so that I can check them without building.

### Rationale and constraints

- Typeset in the preview without a Sphinx build.
