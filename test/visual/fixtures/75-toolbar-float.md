# Research, plan, implement

In the previous chapters we setup an harness around your project to help coding agents be more
effective in discovering and using your codebase.

:::{note}
Remember the role of the harness is to ground the LLM in your project reality, your rules, your
architecture, your sensors and quality gates.
:::

This chapter covers what to put on top of this project harness: the methodology of research, plan,
implement, and how each phase hands a smaller, cleaner context to the next one.
